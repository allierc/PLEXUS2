## Instruction

The standing rules of `INSTRUCTION.md` apply (paper first, the one rule, one change per iteration, the ruler decides, no questions to the human, no commit), plus:

1. **The paper first.** Every number below names its paper, figure and panel, or the published spec and line it is read from. A number no source backs is marked *model choice*.

7. **Cells ARE material.** The MPM twin never builds a vertex mesh, and never sets a cell's shape; the shape is what the points do.

**The clips are not Okuda 2018's equations.** The paper's chemistry is a Gierer-Meinhardt activator-inhibitor with constants `chi` and `gamma` (Eqs. 5-6, 9-10); the clips run Gray-Scott with feed `F` and kill `k`, so the paper's numbers (a spot spacing growing as chi^(1/4), 1.78 between chi 0.1 and 0.01; Fig 4's 341 to 5,295 cells) are not targets of these specs. What carries over is the regime reading of Fig 4d: a patterning rate far above the growth rate gives a low-hysteresis, moving pattern (undulation, Fig 7), one near it gives stable spots that grow into tubes (Fig 5), one far below it gives dilution and a high-hysteresis pattern (Fig 6, branching).

## The equations the specs run (operator docstrings, `diffusion_reaction.py`; each symbol named)

- Gray-Scott per cell (`cell_chem_react[gray_scott]`, `:1485`): `da/dt = r (u a^2 - (F + k) a)`, `du/dt = r (-u a^2 + F (1 - u))`. `a` activator and its own autocatalyst, `u` substrate, both dimensionless; `F` feed rate (tops `u` up to 1 and removes `a`), `k` extra kill rate of `a`, `r` the `rate` multiplier (inverse time).
- Diffusion on the contact graph (`cell_chem_diffuse[graph_laplacian]`): neighbours exchange `a` and `u` at coefficients `d_a` 0.08 and `d_h` 0.16 (published), `chi` 1.3 setting the ratio of the two (the Turing condition is that the inhibiting species spreads faster).
- Growth gated by the activator (`cell_grow`, `:2878`): `ds_j/dt = s_j rate (rho + Hill(a_j))`, `Hill(a) = a^n / (a^n + a_sw^n)`. `s_j` the linear scale of cell j's mechanical targets (volume grows as `s^3`), `rate` the fraction of itself a cell adds per unit time (published 0.000866, or 0.001732 in clips 8 and 9), `rho` the growth with no activator as a fraction of the full rate (0.1, or 0.2 in clips 10-11), `a_sw` the activator at half switch (0.35), `n` = `hill` (4 or 8). A cell divides when its volume reaches `factor` 2.0 times its birth volume (`cell_divide`).
- Curvature feeds the chemistry (`cell_chem_from_shape[curvature]`, `:4321`): `H_j = 2 (mean_k x_k - x_j) . n_j / d_j^2` over the neighbours k of cell j; `x` a cell centroid, `n_j` its outward normal, `d_j` the mean centroid spacing. `H` is about 1/R on a sphere of radius R. It moves the Gray-Scott feed: `F_j = F0 (1 + beta Hhat_j)`, `Hhat` the curvature standardised over the live cells, `F` clamped at zero and at a ceiling (the operator's own blow-up guard), `beta` 0.5, `F0` 0.046 (published); the substrate is nudged by `rate (F_j - F0)(1 - u)`.
- Purse-string tension (`interface_tension`, `:3979`): `E = K_purse sum_{e in interface} l_e`, the sum over mesh edges between an activator-high cell and a low one (high: `a > a_sw a_max`, `a_sw` 0.6, `a_max` the field's running maximum), `l_e` the edge length, `K_purse` 1.0 the line tension (force per length).
- Death (`cell_die`, published `mode: chem_low`, clips 10-11): a cell is marked when its activator on channel `chan` is LOW, below `a_sw` (clip 10: channel 2, the second system's activator, `a_sw` 0.25, from frame 180, at most 0.25 of the cells marked per call; clip 11: the first system's activator, `a_sw` 0.45, from frame 600, at most 0.05); a marked cell older than `min_age` 4 shrinks at `shrink_rate` 0.15 and is removed when it falls to `critical_frac` 0.15 of its size.
- Okuda 2018's own equations (Eqs. 1-10: flux proportional to the boundary area between cells, `dm_j/dt = sum_k mu s_jk (m_k/v_k - m_j/v_j) + v_j R`; Gierer-Meinhardt production `gamma m_A^2 / m_I` and `gamma m_A^2 / (rho_u v_j)`; Hill growth `lambda = lambda_ref (m_A/v)^alpha / (rho_sw^alpha + (m_A/v)^alpha)`; shape energy `U = sum k_v (v - v_eq)^2 / 2 + kappa_s s`) are in `papers/` and are what the clips cite, not what they run.
