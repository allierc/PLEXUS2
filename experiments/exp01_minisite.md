# 01. minisite -- the site's demo movies as record steps

## At a glance

- **Question.** The front matter's: does each card's published spec still run to its picture, and how does the record look change the reading.
- **Time scale.** The spec's own; step 0001 is 2.7 s of SI time in 400 frames.
- **Speaks to.** Cedric; the minisite (`index.qmd`, `gallery/`), whose cards embed the spec they were rendered from.
- **The movie a talk would show.** The card's movie, regenerated.
- **Status / next step.** Step 0001 running (2026-10-27); next cards follow one at a time.
- **Watcher.** `http://127.0.0.1:8835/watch` (PLEXUS_BUILDER=experiments/exp01_minisite, port 8840 + 1 - 6).
- **The record is written by `tools/gui_drive.py opencycle`** (one step per run: spec, png, mp4, why, caption, a journal line); never `Plexus_Main.py -o generate`.

## The papers

None applies: the reference is the minisite itself. Each step names its card (the `sim-name` of the `sim-card` in `index.qmd`) and the gallery movie it regenerates.

## The model

**What exists.** The published spec, unchanged but for its plotting block: `config/minisite/exp01_two_drops_v1.yaml` differs from `config/si_material/si_two_drops3d_s144.yaml` by `general.name`, `plotting.background` (black -> `#8d99a6`), `plotting.foreground` (white, declared), `plotting.dot_size` (1.4 -> 1.0). The physics is not touched.

**Model choices.** The look: `#8d99a6` + white is exp29's record look (the grey the studio's ink look uses); dots at 1.0 so the two balls read as bodies and not as a haze of 1.4-px points.

## The question

- 2026-10-27 12:45 -- The noise-deformed sphere emerges when a frozen, zero-mean, torque-free Perlin force attached to the material points loads an elastic ball against its own stiffness and a velocity drag: the static shape is the balance of the two, its depth set by rho A R / E and its graininess by the noise frequency against R. It is a dynamic balance, not the figure's kinematic displacement of a surface: the same force that dimples also spins a free ball (any residual torque, unresisted) and, past a strain the elastic law holds, tears it (step 8), so amplitude is bounded above where the figure's construction is not.
- 2026-10-27 10:25 -- In the best runs the pictures emerge from the materials alone: two liquid balls under 0.144 N/m surface tension pull each other into one ball; snow cubes crumble because their plastic yield (the operator's fixed 2.5 % compress / 0.75 % stretch) is reached on impact once youngs is 5e4 Pa; water spreads, elastic holds, snow breaks. The site's own black dot movies are the same physics; what differs is only the look, and the 88-line demo's 2D squares became 3D cubes at the demo's centres.
